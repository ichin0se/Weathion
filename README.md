## Weathion

Weathion is a Rust-based TUI application that merges your Notion calendar with real-time weather forecasts, allowing you to quickly access upcoming events and local climate updates in a single terminal interface. Its straightforward layout and command-line approach offer an efficient way to stay on top of your daily schedule and weather outlook without leaving the comfort of your console.
